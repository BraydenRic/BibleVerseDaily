# Credits and licenses

Everything posted is either public domain, openly licensed, or generated by this code. Each
tradition's `tradition.json` records its texts' licenses and sources, and each caption ends with
the attribution line given there.

## Scripture texts

| Text | Used for | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Berean Standard Bible | Christianity, English | Public domain since April 30, 2023 ([terms](https://berean.bible/terms.htm)) | [scrollmapper/bible_databases](https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases) (MIT; notice below). Dashes left spaced where poetry lines were joined ("confidence— but") are closed, as the BSB prints them |
| *The Meaning of the Glorious Koran*, Marmaduke Pickthall (1930) | Islam, English | Public domain: published 1930 (US public domain since 2026); Pickthall died in 1936 | [Project Gutenberg #16955](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16955) (Pickthall paragraphs only). The e-text isn't word for word the 1930 printing, so every scheduled verse was checked against archive.org scans of the 1930 Knopf and Allen & Unwin printings, and 14 verses are restored to the printed wording (listed in `scripts/build_index_quran.py`) |
| The Quran, Uthmani script, Tanzil Project | Islam, Arabic | [Tanzil text license](https://tanzil.net/docs/text_license) (CC BY 3.0): verbatim copies only, with credit and a link to tanzil.net. The full notice is kept in `traditions/islam/texts/arabic.json`, and the text is stored unchanged | [tanzil.net](https://tanzil.net) |
| *The Bhagavad-Gita*, translated by Annie Besant (1895; 4th edition 1922) | Hinduism, English | Public domain: published before 1931; Besant died in 1933 | [Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Bhagavad-Gita_(Besant_4th)) |
| Bhagavad Gita, Sanskrit (Devanagari) as printed in Besant's edition | Hinduism, on screen and in captions | Public domain (4th edition, 1922) | [Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Bhagavad-Gita_(Besant_4th)). Transcription errors (line-break hyphens, slipped spaces, 11 misspellings) are corrected to the standard Gita Press reading (listed in `scripts/build_index_gita.py`) |
| Bhagavad Gita, IAST transliteration | Hinduism, not shown at the moment (kept for an optional romanized caption line) | [Unlicense](https://github.com/gita/gita/blob/main/LICENSE) | [gita/gita](https://github.com/gita/gita) (`data/verse.json` only; its translation files are not used). For 16 verses whose line doesn't match Besant's numbering, the line is generated from the Devanagari with [indic-transliteration](https://github.com/indic-transliteration/indic_transliteration_py) (MIT, used only by the build script) |

The `scripts/build_index_*.py` scripts rebuild each text index from these sources.
